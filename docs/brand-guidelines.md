# TheYarnHook — Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Location:** Bengaluru, India  
**Currency:** ₹ INR  
**Category:** Tactile Soft-Luxury Yarn Studio (Crochet & Knitting)

---

## 1. Brand Voice & Personality
- **Voice Pillars:** Tactile, Unhurried, Knowing, Indian-Rooted.
- **Tone:** Conversational, sensory, specific, never pushy or discount-screaming.
- **Copywriting Rules:**
  - Sentence case for eyebrows, labels, and buttons (never all-caps shouty chrome).
  - Active voice on CTAs (`Shop the collections`, `Add to bag`, `Save changes`).
  - Honest fibre disclosure on every skein: blend, weight (2–7), yardage, needle/hook size.

## 3. Typography Specifications
- **Display Serif:** `Fraunces` (`opsz`, optical-size variable font) — Used for `h1`–`h3`, collection titles, italic poetic taglines.
- **Body Sans:** `Manrope` — Used for UI controls, specs, prices (`tabular-nums`), body paragraphs (base `16px`, line-height `1.5–1.65`).

---

## 4. Logo Usage Rules
- `LogoMark`: Minimum `24px × 24px`, paired with `TheYarnHook` in Fraunces.
- Clearspace: Equal to half the height of the hook mark on all sides.
- Never distort, add drop shadows, or place on low-contrast backgrounds (< 4.5:1).
